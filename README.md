# 🏫 Smart Mess & Hostel Complaint Tracking System

> A data-driven campus management solution that replaces manual complaint registers with a transparent, real-time digital system.

---

## 🚀 Quick Overview (For Judges)
- **Problem:** Mess & hostel complaints are handled via manual registers with zero tracking or transparency.
- **Solution:** A digital complaint system with live submission, centralized records, and data-driven insights.
- **Live MVP:** https://mv1511979-source.github.io/iit-mandi-complaints/
- **Impact:** Faster resolution, accountability, and improved campus management.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement
Traditional complaint handling through physical registers creates a *black hole* for student issues:
- No visibility on complaint status  
- Records can be lost or damaged  
- Repeated issues remain unresolved  
- No data to support administrative decisions  

Students never know whether their complaint was **seen, ignored, or resolved**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution
We developed a **Smart Mess & Hostel Complaint Tracking System** that:
- Digitizes complaint submission
- Stores complaints in a centralized database
- Enables transparent tracking
- Converts grievances into actionable data

The system bridges the gap between **students and administration** through clarity and accountability.

---

## 🧑‍💻 How It Works

### 🎓 Student Side
- Submit complaints via a simple web interface
- Choose category (mess, hostel, hygiene, water, etc.)
- Complaint is logged instantly with structured details

### 🛠️ Admin Side
- All complaints appear in a centralized record
- Track status: *Submitted → In Progress → Resolved*
- Identify repeated or high-frequency issues using data
